How the Final Testnet Season pays

Known limits: one card can serve the programme's work for many identities. The number of requests is fixed, and the chain weighs each identity's stake in the draw up to assignment_stake_cap_multiple × min_stake, a multiple the launch genesis sets to 100, the highest the chain accepts: up to that ceiling an identity's weight is its stake, so splitting a stake into more identities buys no more work, and a larger stake draws a larger share of it; above the ceiling, splitting does buy more. What splitting still buys below it is presence and jury seats: each identity with at least one verified request that day is paid its own presence, and the audit draw seats each identity at most once, so several identities can hold several seats on one programme audit, each paid per consistent verdict. A smaller identity draws a request, and so earns its presence, on fewer days. Read both with dendrad query jobs params -o json (a multiple of 0, or absent, selects the compiled 2; a min_stake of 0 sets no ceiling). A proven window shows an online operator key, not a model.

How the Final Testnet Season pays

    

    
Known limits: one card can serve the programme's work for many identities. The number of requests is fixed, and the chain draws them among present identities by stake, each identity's weight capped at assignment_stake_cap_multiple × min_stake (committee.go::capAssignmentWeights): stake above that ceiling weighs nothing in the draw. Below the ceiling an identity's share follows its stake, and splitting the stake does not raise it; above it, splitting the stake into identities that each still reach the ceiling does, while a split into identities below the ceiling can lower it. At a multiple of 1 every identity bonded at min_stake or more sits at the ceiling: each weighs the same, a larger stake draws no larger share, and splitting a stake into more identities bonded at min_stake draws a larger share of the work. Governance moves the multiple, so which case applies is a reading of the chain, not of this page: GET /dendra/jobs/v1/params on a node's REST API, or dendrad query jobs params -o json (a multiple of 0, or absent, selects the compiled 2; a min_stake of 0, or absent, sets no ceiling). At any multiple, splitting also buys presence and jury seats: each identity with at least one verified request that day is paid its own presence, and the audit draw, which this ceiling does not touch, seats each identity at most once, so several identities can hold several seats on one programme audit, each paid per consistent verdict. An identity that weighs less in the work draw draws a request, and so earns its presence, on fewer days. A proven window shows an online operator key, not a model.
